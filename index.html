How Drawdle works

  1. Hit Start drawing when you're ready — the timer begins.
  2. Sketch the daily prompt before time runs out.
  3. One attempt per day — make it count.
  4. Tap Score drawing to compare your sketch to the reference.
  5. Scoring measures overlap and placement (size-normalized, so draw any size).
  6. Share your score card, streak, and badges.
